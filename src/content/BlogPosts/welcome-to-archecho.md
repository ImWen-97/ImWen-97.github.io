# Welcome to ArchEcho

ArchEcho is my space for documenting ideas from computer architecture research.

I am especially interested in the intersection of **memory systems**, **accelerators**, and **large language model inference**. Papers often present an elegant solution, but the most useful question for me is what assumptions make that solution work—and what changes when the assumptions no longer hold.

This site will gradually collect:

- **Paper Reading:** The research problem, key insight, design choices, assumptions, and open questions.
- **Systems & Architecture:** Notes on Processing-in-Memory, memory bandwidth, KV cache, and reliability.
- **Research Journey:** How I approach experiments, organize ideas, and learn from unexpected results.

This first post introduces the notebook. More detailed technical notes will follow as they are ready to share.
